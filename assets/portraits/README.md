## 表情の順序

- 上段は通常、微笑、心配、驚き、決意。
- 下段は悲しみ、怒り、安堵、会話中、思案。

## 生成・編集プロンプト

> A production portrait expression atlas for a classical hand-painted Japanese high-fantasy game. Exactly five columns and two rows of ten portrait cells. The same person in all ten, wearing the same clothes, with a consistent camera angle and close head-and-shoulders framing. Dark slate background, natural anatomy and painterly gouache and watercolor. In order: neutral, smile, worried, surprised, resolved, sad, angry, relieved, speaking, thoughtful. No captions or borders.

> Allen wears a red scarf and weathered armor. Replace his oversized windblown sweep with a compact chestnut haircut, tapered sides and a short, loose fringe that rests naturally along the head. Keep the same haircut in all expressions.

> Lilith is a clearly feminine adult elf woman with a softly curved oval face, delicate brows and eyelashes, gently defined lips, and naturally parted silver hair falling over her shoulders. Retain her teal cloak, amber clasp, practical outfit and expressive resolve. Keep her features and haircut consistent across all ten expressions.

炉番は暗いフードと銅の留め具、灯台守は海風で荒れた顔と藍色の外套、拓本師は陶土色の頭巾と石粉色の前掛け、バルドは銀髭・古傷・紫の外套を手がかりにしました。住民の4役は島の町で使っているスプライトに合わせています。
